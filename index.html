04 / Method

How I work.

S.01

Discover

I talk to users and read the data before I write anything down. I look for the problem behind the request.

Artifact: notes + problem statement
S.02

Define

I turn findings into a sharp scope: who it serves, what success looks like, what is out. Ambiguity dies here.

Artifact: PRD, success metrics
S.03

Design

I sketch flows with the team, cut ruthlessly, and keep the smallest version that still solves the problem.

Artifact: flows + prototypes
S.04

Deliver

I run the backlog in priority order, unblock engineers daily, and ship in small, reviewable pieces.

Artifact: shipped releases
S.05

Measure

I check what changed after release against the success metric, then decide: iterate, keep, or kill.

Artifact: readout + decision

PRD excerpt · sample

Task assignment and progress tracking

Problem

Team members could not see who owned a task or what was still outstanding, so coordination gaps slowed delivery.

Goals and non-goals

Goal: every task has a visible owner and status. Non-goal: time tracking or performance scoring.

Users

Team members coordinating delivery; stakeholders checking progress.

Requirements
  • P0: assign owner, set status, filter by owner.
  • P1: overdue highlighting, weekly digest.
Success metrics

Fewer "who is doing this" threads; tasks closed per week.

Risks

Adoption drops if updating status feels like extra work.

Open questions

Do we need per-task comments, or is status enough?
